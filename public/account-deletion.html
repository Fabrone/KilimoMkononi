Request Deletion of Your Kilimo Mkononi Account

To permanently delete your Kilimo Mkononi account and all associated personal data (profile information, farm records, education module data if applicable, etc.), please send a deletion request via email.

Email to: [your-current-email-here, e.g. info@jvalmacis.com or your personal Gmail]
Recommended subject: Kilimo Mkononi Account Deletion Request

Include the following in your email to help us verify and process quickly:
  • The email address used to register in the Kilimo Mkononi app
  • Your full name as registered in the app
  • Your registered phone number (if provided)
  • Your county, constituency, or ward (for faster identity verification)
  • Optional: Approximate date you created the account or any other identifying details

Note: Certain anonymized or legally required data may be retained, but all personally identifiable information will be removed.

If you have questions about this process, reply to the same email address.
